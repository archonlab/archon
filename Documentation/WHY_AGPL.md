# Why Project ARCHON uses the AGPL

Project ARCHON is intended to remain an open scientific platform while allowing
commercial licensing under separately negotiated terms.

The GNU Affero General Public License version 3 was selected because ARCHON can
be distributed as software and can also be modified and operated through a
network interface. Like the GPL, the AGPL requires corresponding source in
certain distributions of covered object code. Its section 13 additionally
requires an operator of a modified network-interactive version to offer the
corresponding source to users who interact with that version over the network.

That network provision supports reproducibility and gives the community access
to modifications used to provide a service, in the circumstances defined by the
license.

## What the AGPL does not mean

The AGPL is not a non-commercial license. It permits commercial use, sale, and
paid services when its terms are followed.

It also does not automatically:

- require publication of unmodified private use;
- apply to every independent program that communicates with ARCHON;
- license scientific discoveries, user data, or every output produced by the
  software;
- change the licenses of third-party components or content; or
- grant rights in Project ARCHON names, logos, or branding beyond legally
  permitted descriptive use.

The exact legal boundary of a covered or combined work depends on the facts and
applicable law. Project documentation cannot expand or contract that boundary.

## Why commercial licensing still exists

An organization may prefer a negotiated commercial license when it wants to
combine Project Materials with proprietary code, distribute a closed-source
product, operate a modified proprietary service without complying with AGPL
source obligations, or obtain contractual support and assurances.

Commercial licensing is an alternative grant from the Project Licensor. It does
not weaken or revoke the AGPL rights already granted to an AGPL recipient.

See [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md) for the project-wide scope notice and
[`COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md`](COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md) for commercial-licensing
information. The complete AGPLv3 text in the repository-root `LICENSE` file is
controlling.
