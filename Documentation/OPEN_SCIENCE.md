# Project ARCHON open-science principles

Project ARCHON exists to advance reproducible research into complex systems,
emergent behavior, and computational worlds.

We aim to make research performed with ARCHON:

- reproducible from recorded rules, parameters, seeds, software versions, and
  experimental conditions;
- transparent about provenance, uncertainty, failed tests, competing
  explanations, and limitations;
- reviewable through preserved evidence and links to canonical records;
- accessible through open methods, interoperable formats, and clear
  documentation where law, ethics, privacy, and third-party rights permit;
- careful not to turn an attractive visualization or automated summary into an
  unsupported scientific claim.

The project welcomes collaboration from universities, research institutes,
independent scientists, students, and citizen scientists.

## Software, data, and results are different

These principles are not a software or data license and do not add conditions to
the GNU Affero General Public License.

- Project ARCHON's first-party software and other Project Materials are licensed
  as described in [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md).
- Third-party software and content retain their own licenses.
- Researchers retain whatever rights they hold in their original data,
  discoveries, and results. Output is not automatically licensed under the AGPL
  merely because ARCHON produced it.
- A dataset or publication is open only under the license expressly attached to
  that dataset or publication. Contributors must not publish personal,
  confidential, restricted, or third-party data without authority.

When publishing research, we encourage authors to release the smallest complete
reproducibility package that can lawfully be shared: software version, rules,
configuration, seeds, protocols, analysis steps, machine-readable results, and
known limitations. Choose and state an appropriate license for any released
data and writing rather than relying on this principles document.

## Relationship to commercial licensing

Commercial licensing supports continued development and may provide different
software-license terms. It does not transfer ownership of a customer's research
or create a claim over scientific discoveries. Confidentiality, publication,
and data-use obligations exist only when stated in a separate signed agreement.

Open science depends on honest provenance as much as open access. Do not remove
authorship, third-party notices, negative results, or uncertainty in the name of
simplification.
