## When a commercial license may be appropriate

Contact the Project Licensor before using Project ARCHON if you want terms that
permit, for example:

- integration of Project Materials into proprietary software;
- distribution of a closed-source modified or combined product;
- operation of a modified proprietary network service without satisfying the

- embedding Project Materials in a commercial product under negotiated terms;

Commercial activity by itself does not require a commercial license. The AGPL
permits commercial use, provided its terms are followed. Whether a particular
architecture creates a covered or combined work is fact-specific and is not
decided by this summary.

## Scope of a commercial agreement

Each commercial agreement must identify at least:

- the customer and licensed Project ARCHON version or materials;
- permitted users, installations, products, services, and distribution models;
- whether affiliates, contractors, hosting providers, or end users are covered;
- source-code, modification, redistribution, and sublicensing rights;
- term, territory, fees, support, updates, and termination consequences;
- treatment of third-party components and their non-waivable license terms;
- warranty, liability, confidentiality, data-protection, export-control, and
  governing-law terms as applicable.

No commercial agreement can remove obligations imposed by a third party's
license. Project ARCHON can commercially license only rights owned or controlled
by the Project Licensor.
